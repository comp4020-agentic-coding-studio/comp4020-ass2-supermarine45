# Reflections

1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?
The breakthrough that moved the work forward is the proofreading adn editing of each of the content/pages manually. Initially, the agent had produced all components of the prototype as requested. However, most of these were generally "AI slop". Though I tried to iteratively improve the content through prompt, I had to eventually replace these contents manually. Only then is the curriculum coherent.

2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?
After this assignment, I realized the importance of reviewing the work produced by an agent. In the end, each piece of work carries the accountabiility of the software developer. It is crucial, therefore, to ensure the generated contents are not mere "AI slop".
