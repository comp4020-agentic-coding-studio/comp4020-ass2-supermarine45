**Core Theory:** *Scaling Household Purchasing Algorithms to Event Logistics* (Fenn, Journal of Applied Consumer Logistics, 2024).

## The Lecture

This final seminar marks the culmination of the SLOP4230 curriculum. An economic optimization that exists solely within a Python terminal is a failure of applied logistics. We must now scale the individual purchasing algorithms generated in Week 10 to satisfy the physical, macro-event requirements of a 50-person cohort. 

This requires bridging the gap between digital index maximization and physical supply chain realities. We will calculate vehicle payload constraints, standardizing the volumetric displacement of bulk cask transport against the suspension limits of standard passenger vehicles. Students will finalize their mathematically optimal beverage portfolios, preparing to formally defend their specific exploitation of the WET loophole, spatial retail anomalies, and combinatorial optimization constraints before the faculty.

## Applied Computational Lab

In this final workshop, you will conduct a full physical and financial audit of your Capstone Event Portfolio prior to submission. You will link your finalized CSV dataset to a physical payload modeling spreadsheet. 

Your task is to calculate the gross tonnage and spatial volume of your optimized inventory, and distribute it mathematically across a simulated convoy of standard domestic vehicles. An algorithm that successfully maximizes the standard drink yield but inadvertently exceeds the legal axle weight limit of a Toyota Camry will be penalized for logistical failure. You must prove that your inventory can physically transition from the retail floor plan (mapped in Week 7) to the event space.

## Mandatory Literature

- [Implementation of effective alcohol control strategies is needed at large sports and entertainment events](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1753-6405.2011.00813.x)
  — Lyne & Galloway, *Australian and New Zealand Journal of Public Health* 36(1), 2012, 55–60. 
  This paper provides empirical observations of thirteen large-scale entertainment events across nine venues. Pay strict attention to the authors' findings regarding the operational friction of serving four drinks per transaction rather than two. This is a quantified, per-transaction constraint measured in the field. It represents exactly the kind of physical bottleneck your Event Portfolio must mathematically plan around, rather than discovering through operational failure on the day of the event.

- [Interventions in sports settings to reduce risky alcohol consumption and alcohol-related harm: a systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-016-0183-y)
  — Kingsland, Wiggers, Vashum, Hodder & Wolfenden, *Systematic Reviews* 5(1), 2016. 
  A systematic review delineating which event-scale interventions actually possess an evidence base for mitigating harm, and which are merely performative. As established in Week 11, your hyper-optimized inventory will generate significant externalities. Your final capstone presentation is structurally and academically stronger if your externality mitigation strategy explicitly incorporates a proven intervention documented in this review, rather than relying on unverified assumptions.
