**Core reading:** *Scaling Household Purchasing Algorithms to Event
Logistics* (Journal of Applied Consumer Logistics, 2024)

The culmination of the course. We scale individual purchasing algorithms
up to macro-event logistics. Students will calculate vehicle payload
weights for bulk cask transport and finalize their mathematically
optimal beverage portfolios for a 50-person cohort, defending their
calculated tax loopholes.

*Key Concept:* Supply chain logistics, payload mathematics, and economic
synthesis.

## Mandatory reading

- [Implementation of effective alcohol control strategies is needed at large sports and entertainment events](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1753-6405.2011.00813.x)
  — Lyne & Galloway, *Australian and New Zealand Journal of Public Health*
  36(1), 2012, 55–60. Thirteen events at nine venues, observed. Note the
  finding on serving four drinks per transaction rather than two: that is
  a per-transaction constraint, measured in the field, of exactly the
  kind your portfolio has to plan around rather than discover on the day.
- [Interventions in sports settings to reduce risky alcohol consumption and alcohol-related harm: a systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-016-0183-y)
  — Kingsland, Wiggers, Vashum, Hodder & Wolfenden, *Systematic Reviews*
  5(1), 2016. Which event-scale interventions have evidence behind them
  and which do not. Your externality statement is stronger for naming one
  that does.
