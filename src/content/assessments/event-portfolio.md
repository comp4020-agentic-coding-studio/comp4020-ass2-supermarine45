## The brief

The capstone project. Design the mathematically optimal, cheapest
possible beverage inventory for a 50-person cohort. You must maximize
total standard drinks while minimizing financial outlay, heavily relying
on the tax-loophole efficiencies and spatial pricing anomalies identified
in earlier weeks.

## What you submit

A 10-slide presentation deck and a linked, fully costed budget
spreadsheet detailing the logistical breakdown of how the volume will be
transported from the retail floor to the event space (factoring in
vehicle payload weights).
