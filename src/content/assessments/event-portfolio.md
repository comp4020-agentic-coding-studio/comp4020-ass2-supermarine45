## The brief

This is the final assessment of the SLOP4230 curriculum. Your objective is to design and justify a cost-efficient beverage inventory for a 50-person cohort. The inventory should maximise total standard-drink yield while minimising financial cost.

Using the live dataset collected in Week 9, apply the taxation frameworks and retail pricing concepts covered throughout the course, including the WET system and observed pricing differences.

Your analysis must also consider logistical feasibility. Model the physical transport of the inventory to the event space, including vehicle payload limits and available storage volume. The final proposal should demonstrate that the selected inventory is both economically and physically feasible.


## What you submit

You will submit two items:

* A **presentation deck** of at least 10 slides, exported as a PDF.
* A **fully costed budget spreadsheet** in `.xlsx` or `.csv` format.

The presentation must clearly explain your optimisation strategy, taxation considerations, and transport calculations.

The spreadsheet must list each SKU, unit price, ABV-to-dollar ratio, and cumulative standard-drink yield. Include a separate sheet documenting the transport and payload calculations.

Review the `spec` carefully before submitting.
