## Learning objectives

By the end of this lecture you should be able to:

- define price elasticity of demand against discount depth
- state the elasticity gap between RTDs and wine or beer
- explain that gap in terms of loyalty and substitutability
- say why deeper, more frequent discounting is rational here

---

## The measurement

Hold the discount depth constant. Measure the volume response.

| Category | Relative elasticity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ready-to-drink cans | ~2× |
| Packaged beer | baseline |
| Wine | baseline |

RTDs move **roughly double** the volume that wine or beer moves under the
same discount.

---

## Why this category

Two causes, per the reading:

- **weak brand loyalty** — the category is young, and its brands are not
  strongly differentiated in the way beer's are
- **easy substitution** — at a fixed standard-drink price, one RTD is a
  very close substitute for another

Neither is true of wine, where varietal and region do real work in the
purchase decision.

# The volume response pays for the discount

---

## The retailer's arithmetic

- a deeper discount cuts per-unit margin
- in an elastic category, volume rises by more than margin falls
- so the discount is **profitable**, not a loss leader
- and the deeper and more frequent it is, the more it earns

This is why week 4's tax structure cannot, on its own, explain the
promotional pattern you see in the aisle.

---

## What this means for your index

- an RTD's index moves a lot, and moves often
- a single observation of that price is a snapshot of a cycle
- **timestamp every price you record**, from this week onward
- week 9's scrape depends on this habit existing already

---

## Common errors

- estimating elasticity from price alone, with no volume data, and
  reporting it as though it were measured
- mistaking a recurring promotion for a structural repricing
- assuming a large percentage discount moves the index by as much — RTD
  cans carry few standard drinks per unit, so it usually does not
- generalising a single retailer's promotional calendar to the category
