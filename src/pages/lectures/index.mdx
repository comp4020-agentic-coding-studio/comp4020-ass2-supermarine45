Twelve weeks, one argument: a retail price is production cost, plus
category-specific tax, plus margin, plus whatever the shelf position is
worth — and each of those four terms can be measured separately.

Weeks 1 to 3 establish the baseline the other three sit on top of. Weeks 4
to 6 add taxation and set the ad valorem Wine Equalisation Tax against
volumetric excise. Weeks 7 and 8 add the floor plan and the display on it.
Weeks 9 and 10 turn the whole thing into a dataset and then into an
optimisation. Week 11 asks what that optimisation costs, and week 12 scales
it to an event.

Every week names the peer-reviewed work its claims come from, and opens by
saying what it inherits from the week before. Where a week has slides, they
are linked from that week's page.
