# Policies and support

These rules apply across every assessment in Retail Ethanol Economics.
Where a brief needs a rule restated, it links back to this page rather than
repeating it.

## Academic integrity

**Algorithmic plagiarism.** The ABV-to-dollar optimisation script you use
for weeks 7 through 12, including the Event Portfolio's constrained
inventory, must be your own. Submitting a third party's or a classmate's
scraping or optimisation script as your own work is assessed as plagiarism
under the standard university policy, and it defeats the specific learning
outcome those weeks are built around: that you can derive and defend the
optimisation yourself, not that you can produce a correct-looking output.

**Data provenance.** The dataset behind the Data Scraping Exercise must be
scraped live during the current semester. Submitting a previous cohort's
dataset, or a dataset scraped by someone else, is treated as fabrication
regardless of whether the numbers themselves are accurate — the exercise
assesses the scraping method, not only its result.

**Scraping conduct.** Automated collection must respect a minimum
two-second crawl delay per request against any retailer's site, and must
stop entirely if a site's terms of service or `robots.txt` prohibit
scraping. A dataset that could only have been collected by disregarding
either constraint will not be accepted, irrespective of its completeness.

## Late work and extensions

Extensions are granted for documented illness or misadventure, requested
before the due date wherever possible. A request made after a deadline has
passed needs a reason the deadline itself could not have accommodated.
Work submitted late without an approved extension is penalised under the
standard university late-submission schedule.

## Physiological liability

The economic models this course teaches are mathematically sound within
the assumptions each week states. Slop University accepts no legal,
medical, or physiological responsibility should a student attempt to
physically consume the inventory designed for the Event Portfolio, or any
other mathematically optimised purchasing decision produced in this
course. The index measures value; it does not measure a safe quantity to
drink, and week 11's externality framework exists specifically because the
two are not the same calculation.

## Getting help

Ask during sessions first — most questions about a specific week's
material or a piece of in-progress work are answered faster there than by
email. Email a convenor or tutor directly for anything that cannot wait
until the next session, using the contact details on the
[people page](/people/).
