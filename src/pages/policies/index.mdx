# Policies and support

These rules apply across every assessment in Retail Ethanol Economics.
Where a brief needs a rule restated, it links back to this page rather than
repeating it.

## Academic integrity

All students must strictly adhere to the SlopU Academic Honesty
Framework. Due to the quantitative and data-driven nature of this course,
highly specific policies apply to your coursework. Algorithmic
plagiarism—specifically, utilizing unauthorized third-party scripts or
historical datasets to calculate your ABV-to-dollar index—constitutes
severe academic misconduct and will result in an immediate zero. Your
Week 9 dataset must be scraped live during the current semester.
Furthermore, while the economic models designed in this course are
mathematically sound, Slop University accepts no legal, medical, or
physiological liability should students attempt to physically consume
their mathematically optimized capstone inventory designs. The models
are strictly theoretical.

## Late work and extensions

Retail markets are volatile; therefore, academic deadlines are absolute.
Late submissions will incur a penalty of 10% of the total available
marks per calendar day. Submissions received more than five days after
the deadline will receive a mark of zero. If you require an extension
due to extenuating medical or personal circumstances, you must apply via
the SlopU Special Consideration portal at least 48 hours prior to the
assessment deadline, accompanied by appropriate documentation.

## Getting help

For technical issues regarding data scraping (e.g., bypassing basic rate
limits or HTTP 429 Too Many Requests errors), attend the weekly computer
lab sessions on Thursdays. For theoretical clarifications regarding
taxation law or fermentation kinetics, utilize the SLOP4230 Discussion
Forum. The course convenors will monitor the forum and respond within 48
business hours. Do not email the convenors directly with debugging
questions.
