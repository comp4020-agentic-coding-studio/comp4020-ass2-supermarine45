This course requires three assessment items, totaling 100 marks. Each piece is designed to evaluate a distinct component of our economic methodology, with deadlines deliberately scheduled for the week immediately following the relevant theoretical lectures.

* The **WET Policy Brief** tests your ability to articulate the mechanics of volume-agnostic taxation.
* The **Data Scraping Exercise** evaluates your technical capacity to construct a retail dataset and accurately compute the ABV-to-dollar index across every compiled SKU.
* The **Event Portfolio** serves as the capstone. It asks you to mathematically define aninventory that is fundamentally optimal, not merely cheap, while clearly articulating the logistics of that optimization.

Every assignment brief includes a `spec`: the definitive list of criteria your marker will check. Read it before you begin your draft, and read it again before you submit. This list does not change, and it is deliberately the only place where technical requirements and deliverables are stated. Assumptions made outside the scope of the `spec` will not be graded.
