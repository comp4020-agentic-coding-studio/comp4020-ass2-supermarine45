Three pieces. Each assesses a different part of the method, and each falls
the week after the lectures it depends on.

The **WET Policy Brief** asks whether you can make the tax argument in
prose, to a citation standard. The **Data Scraping Exercise** asks whether
you can build a dataset large enough to trust and compute the index
correctly across all of it. The **Event Portfolio** asks whether you can
defend an inventory that is optimal rather than merely cheap — and whether
you can say plainly what that optimisation costs.

Every brief carries a `spec`: the list of things a marker checks. Read it
before you start and again before you submit. It is the same list both
times, and it is deliberately the only place the requirements are stated.
