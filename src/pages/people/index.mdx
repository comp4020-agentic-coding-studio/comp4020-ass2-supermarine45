Two people teach this course, and the split is not arbitrary. Marisol Quaye
designed the twelve-week arc and takes the odd weeks — production cost,
margin structure, taxation and the ethics of the thing. Idris Fenn takes
the even weeks, where the argument moves from the ledger to the shop floor,
and runs the labs in between.

Email for anything course-level. For feedback on work in progress, the lab
is faster than an inbox.
