The course is taught by two instructors. Dr. Marisol Quaye teaches in odd weeks, covering the theoretical foundations of the market, including production costs, margins, taxation, and bioethical considerations.

Dr. Idris Fenn teaches in even weeks, applying these concepts to real-world settings and leading the weekly computational labs.

For administrative questions, policy clarifications, or general course matters, contact the convenors by email. For feedback on mathematical models, data-scraping scripts, or portfolio work, the weekly lab sessions are the preferred option.
